# Business requirements

## Problem statement
A fictional financial institution wants a transparent view of synthetic transaction activity, data-quality exceptions and monitoring alerts for an educational demonstration.

## Users
- Analytics specialist: explores transaction patterns and validates data.
- Compliance operations manager: reviews alert volumes, severity and scenario distribution.
- Data owner: monitors completeness and referential integrity.

## Questions answered
- How many transactions and customers were generated?
- Which illustrative monitoring scenarios generated alerts?
- What data-quality exceptions were identified?
- How do alerts distribute by severity and customer risk band?

## Non-goals
This project does not identify criminals, make suspicious activity reports, perform actual sanctions screening, replace a bank's transaction monitoring system, or provide legal/regulatory advice.
